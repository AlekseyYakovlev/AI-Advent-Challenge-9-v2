<objective>
Make every modal close only through an explicit control inside it: remove the four backdrop-click closers (and the global Escape closer) from `ui/static/app.js`, lock the rule with a Node-free source guard test, and prove the behaviour in a real browser on an isolated copy of the app.

Purpose: Phase 10 goal (ROADMAP, promoted from backlog 999.5): "a modal closes only when its 'x' is clicked; clicking outside the modal (on the backdrop) must not close it". Today a stray click next to the Settings or Scheduler form discards whatever the user typed.
Output: edited `ui/static/app.js` (deletion only plus one comment), a non-visual attribute fix in `ui/static/index.html`, new `tests/test_modal_close_policy.py`, two one-line doc notes, a recorded browser UAT result.
</objective>
