<objective>
Wire retrieval into the chat turn as a deterministic, fail-soft pre-step: load the chat's RAG config, retrieve, budget and merge the fragments block into the outbound copy only, persist rag_sources on the assistant message, and add done.rag to the final frame.

Purpose: this is the core RAG behavior (RAG-02..RAG-05); the risky seams are budget vs. compression and failures vs. the user-message-deleting error paths.
Output: agent/rag_turn.py, three touch points in agent/ws.py, tests/test_rag_turn.py, tests/test_rag_ws.py.
</objective>
